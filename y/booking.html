About the Movie

A spirited, outspoken high-school girl, Pooja Mathew, falls head over heels for Giri, an older organic farmer and kung-fu practitioner. Unapologetic in her pursuit, she spends years hoping and planning to win his affection. Directed by Jude Anthany Joseph, this blockbuster romantic comedy is cherished for its delightful humor, soulful music by Shaan Rahman, and an unforgettable portrayal of youth, love, and persistence.
