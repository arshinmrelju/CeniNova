About the Movie

A Blockbuster Classic

Bunny, a happy-go-lucky pizza delivery boy, crosses paths with Madhumathi, the daughter of a strict politician who dreams of completing her MBBS. Circumstances lead them into an unexpected marriage of convenience, sparking a hilarious and emotional journey. Directed by A. Karunakaran, this blockbuster entertainer is cherished for its chartbuster music by Yuvan Shankar Raja, electric dance numbers, and the unforgettable chemistry between Allu Arjun and Genelia.
